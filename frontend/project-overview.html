LIFETAG — HOSPITAL MANAGEMENT SYSTEM

Lifetag centralizes hospital operations — patient records, doctor scheduling, appointments, billing, blood bank, and bed occupancy — into one role-based system for Admin, Doctor, Reception, and Patient users. Built as a capstone project.

CAPSTONE BUILD · 2026 10 MODULES · 4 ROLES
MODULES OVERVIEW
1

User Authentication & Roles

Login, role-based access (Admin / Doctor / Reception / Patient), real username & password authentication backed by a MySQL database with BCrypt-hashed passwords and JWT session tokens.

2

Dashboard

Overview stats, recent appointments, ward occupancy, quick links to every module.

3

Patient Management

Register/update patient records, blood group, address, assigned doctor.

4

Doctor Management

Manage doctor profiles, specialization, experience, duty status.

5

Appointment Management

Link patients to doctors, schedule date/time, track status end-to-end.

6

Room & Bed Management

Ward-wise bed grid, live occupancy, assign/discharge patients per bed.

7

Billing & Payment

Multi-item invoices with live totals, payment status tracking.

8

Insurance

Empanelled provider directory, patient policy records, claim submission and Admin approve/reject workflow.

9

Blood Bank Management

Stock by blood group with low/critical alerts, donor and request records.

10

Feedback & Reports

Patient ratings and comments, plus dashboard analytics and exportable summaries.

11

Government Schemes

Scheme directory (PM-JAY, CGHS, ESI, state schemes), Aadhaar/beneficiary-card enrollment with an Admin eligibility-verification gate, and package-rate claim submission through to government sanction.
